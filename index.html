Weather Regimes — Grams¶

Analysis and replication of Christian Grams’ year-round North Atlantic–European weather regime classification.

The dataset is published on Zenodo: Grams (2025). It covers 7 weather regimes (AT, ZO, ScTr, AR, EuBL, ScBL, GL) derived from ERA5 reanalysis at 3-hourly resolution from 1950 to the present.

How to read this book¶

Each chapter is an executed notebook produced by the Snakemake pipeline from a script in pipeline/.

References¶
